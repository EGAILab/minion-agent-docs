## Design notes
**Settlement.**
- The run waits for the process's exit, then for EOF on both pipes or 100 ms without further data. Each chunk re-arms that window.
- At settlement it calls `ReadableStream.close()` on stdout and stderr, never `terminate()`.
  - A background job (`cmd &`) survives the command, and its next write meets a closed reader.
  - Late output is not part of the result. This is Pi's `child.stdout?.destroy(); child.stderr?.destroy()`.

**Lookup.**
- Read pumps run concurrently against one 1 MiB budget over both pipes; the crossing chunk is kept.
- The timer is 5000 ms.
- Collection ends at exit plus EOF on both pipes, with no grace period.
- An unexited lookup is interrupted through `terminate()` (`DIV-001`). An exited lookup keeps its status (`C002`).
- The streams are closed when collection ends.

**Decoding.**
- One CPython incremental `utf-8-sig` decoder with `errors="replace"` runs over the merged chunks of both pipes. This is WHATWG UTF-8 with exactly one leading BOM stripped, even when the BOM is split across chunks.
- The rolling tail and the line-boundary flag follow Pi's `OutputAccumulator`.
- `truncated`, `truncatedBy` and the totals come from the whole stream.

**Full-output file.**
- It is created through `ctx.fs` when the stream first needs it, back-filled with every earlier raw chunk, then appended.
- It holds the raw bytes, never decoded text.
- If the file cannot be written, the run ends (Minion-defined).

**Precheck order** follows Pi's `exec`: timeout validation, then abort, then shell selection, then cwd. The cwd check is `probe_dir_entry` on POSIX and `file_info` on Windows (`CE-WP133-01`).

**Classification:** the signal first, then the timeout, then the exit code. `"(no output)"` is the empty text, including after a non-zero exit (`WP133-CON-R001`).

**Timer:** `max(1, trunc(ms))` (`WP133-CON-R003`).

**Session environment (Owner Q1).** Variables are removed by exact spelling; a case variant survives. They are then re-injected from the call's `ToolExecutionContext`. Absent fields are never injected as `"none"` or `""`.

**Command projection (`WP133-AUD-R001`).** The scalar form is computed before either transport: an unpaired surrogate becomes U+FFFD.

**Test hardening during implementation.** Two settlement witnesses were timed and could flake under load (a descendant could start after the 100 ms grace). They are now event-driven: the parent exits only once its descendant runs. The flake had once masked a control's real killer; after the change every control's killer was re-checked on both platforms.

The spec's witness list (WP-13.3 "Witnesses") maps onto `test_bash.py`, `test_bash_shell.py`, `test_bash_output.py` and the canonical scenarios. L12-D002 §16.5 #12 (the background-job witness) is `test_bash.py::test_a_background_job_survives_settlement_and_meets_released_pipes`.

**Negative controls** (`scripts/wp133_bash_negative_controls.py`). Each fault is applied to a temporary copy of `src`, and the run must fail a behavioural witness:
- precheck order (×2);
- timeout-first classification;
- settling on `wait()` alone;
- EOF without the grace period;
- a decoder per stream;
- resetting the decoder per chunk;
- no BOM strip;
- decoded text in the full-output file;
- truncation computed from the tail;
- status before the truncation notice;
- `""` as the empty text of a non-zero exit;
- always or never dropping the partial first line;
- raw or rounded timer milliseconds;
- an unbounded or per-stream lookup budget;
- a lookup settling at exit or after an idle grace;
- a lookup failing on any interruption (the `C002` control);
- a direct-`SIGTERM` lookup interruption (the `DIV-001` control);
- a lookup keeping its pipes;
- `canonical_path`- or `exists`-based existence;
- a following Windows cwd check;
- case-insensitive `MINION_*` removal;
- injecting `"none"` or `""` for absent fields;
- no command projection, or a surrogateescape projection;
- `bash` using `terminate()` to release pipes, and `bash` keeping its pipes (L12-D002 §16.6).

Two listed controls have no injectable seam, and the script reports them as structural:
- **a per-chunk BOM strip:** covered by the decoder-reset fault;
- **partial updates emitted:** the definition takes no update callback (Q2), and the `test_zero_partial_updates_with_the_final_result` witness covers it.
